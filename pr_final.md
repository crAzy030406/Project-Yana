# 01. Project Overview

## Project Name

**ProjectYaana**

## Category

Clipping Agency / Creator Growth Initiative / Short-Form Content Distribution

## Current Focus

**Gaming creators**

## Core Concept

ProjectYaana is a clipping agency built specifically to help **small and aspiring creators turn their existing long-form content into multiple pieces of short-form content and distribute that content to new audiences.**

The project is currently focused primarily on gaming because the founders have a strong personal connection to the gaming industry and have collectively spent approximately **6–7 years around gaming and content creation**.

ProjectYaana is not positioned simply as a video editing service.

The larger idea is:

> **Help smaller creators get discovered through short-form content and give them the distribution they need to become the next generation of recognizable creators.**

The agency takes existing long-form content, identifies moments with potential, converts those moments into short-form clips, and creates a scalable clipping workflow around the creator.

---

# 02. The Problem

The creator ecosystem has become increasingly difficult for new creators to enter.

There are already established creators who have:

* Large audiences
* Existing communities
* Strong distribution
* Years of accumulated content
* Established personal brands
* Algorithmic recognition
* Sponsorship relationships

This creates a difficult environment for someone who has recently started creating content.

A new creator may produce genuinely good long-form content but still struggle to get discovered.

The problem is therefore not always:

> "The creator does not have good content."

Often the problem is:

> **The content is not reaching enough people.**

Long-form content is particularly difficult for newer creators because viewers already have countless established creators competing for their attention.

Short-form content creates another entry point.

A viewer may never intentionally search for a small creator's 90-minute video.

They may, however, discover that creator through:

* A 30-second gaming moment
* A funny interaction
* A strong opinion
* A surprising reaction
* A clutch gameplay moment
* A useful insight
* A controversial statement
* A highly relatable situation
* A memorable personality moment

That short-form piece can become the first interaction between the viewer and the creator.

# 03. The Opportunity

# 09. What Is Clipping?

Clipping is the process of identifying valuable or entertaining moments inside long-form content and turning them into short-form videos.

For example:

```text
2-hour gaming stream
        ↓
Identify strong moments
        ↓
Funny moment
Clutch moment
Reaction
Story
Opinion
Interaction
        ↓
Short-form clips
        ↓
Social distribution
```

The objective is not to randomly cut a long video into pieces.

The objective is to identify moments that can stand on their own while preserving enough context for the viewer to understand why the moment matters.

---

# 10. Why Short-Form?

Short-form content creates a lower-friction discovery mechanism.

A viewer can encounter a creator without already knowing who that creator is.

This is particularly valuable for small creators.

The viewer may:

1. Discover the clip.
2. Enjoy the moment.
3. Recognize the creator.
4. Visit the creator's profile.
5. Watch additional content.
6. Become a follower/viewer.
7. Eventually become part of the creator's community.

The clip therefore acts as an introduction.
