The Bottleneck

You don't have a content problem.
You have a distribution bottleneck.

The Discovery Barrier

Established creators already dominate algorithmic recommendations. A new creator can produce incredible 2-hour gameplay streams, but new viewers rarely search for an unknown creator's 90-minute upload.

The Short-Form Gateway

Short-form content creates a zero-friction discovery layer. A 30-second clutch gameplay moment or relatable reaction introduces you to thousands of viewers who would never have clicked your long-form video first.

Moment Extraction

One stream holds dozens of breakthrough moments.

We identify the exact clutch plays, unexpected humor, and strong opinions that resonate on social feeds.

What We Do

We turn long-form creator content into short-form distribution built for discovery.

End-to-End Clipping Infrastructure

ProjectYaana is not simply a video editing service. We build and manage the entire workflow: identifying highlights, editing for hook retention, coordinating clippers, and distributing across channels.

Accessible to Emerging Creators

Big creator agencies charge exorbitant fees reserved for massive influencers. ProjectYaana was created specifically to give smaller and aspiring creators the distribution systems they need to compete.

The Workflow
01

Content & System Setup

You provide your existing long-form streams, recordings, or podcasts. We set up the internal campaign architecture, define clipping parameters, and establish distribution schedules.

02

Moment Extraction & Production

Our team identifies moments with high emotional, humorous, or gameplay value. We edit vertical clips optimized for viewer retention, crisp typography, and narrative context that stands on its own.

03

Multi-Platform Distribution

Clips are scheduled across short-form feeds (YouTube Shorts, Reels, TikTok). Each clip serves as a gateway bringing new viewers back to your primary channel and growing community.
